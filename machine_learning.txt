Machine Learning is a discipline of computer science focused on creating algorithms that allow computers to learn from and make predictions based on data. I am particularly interested in supervised learning techniques, which involve training models on labeled datasets to make accurate predictions. Exploring this area will help me understand how AI applications, like recommendation systems and autonomous vehicles function. However, there are some complications with this. The person who creates the training models and data sets for the computer to examine will unknowingly create bias in the machines learning process. This is a problem that I hope to one day be able to solve.

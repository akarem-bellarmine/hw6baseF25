I am interested in environmentalism as it is related to computer science. I know that might sound strange, but here is my reasoning: AI is booming now. It is going through a fast evolution and it is taking up more computing space, energy, industrial output, and consumer electronics than anything I've seen in years. OpenAI has purchased over 40% of the RAM from the entire market for the next 4 years. That includes consumer RAM as well. This means we are at an all time low of consumer electronics, and an all time high of industrial output and waste. Data centers are popping up all over the US and people are protesting because of the pollution it puts out in such high quantities. There was supposed to be a data center put in near Prospect (where I live), however the city protested and they relented. The data center was not built. But in other unlucky cities where they are being built, they are experiencing mass amounts of pollution and industrial waste due to the data centers' output. If we want to protect the planet and also have our AI, something needs to be done, and soon. I would like to be a consultant or environmental advocate for a business focused on protecting the environment. I wouldn't mind working for a company that does use AI, as long as my role was to help that company become more sustainable.
